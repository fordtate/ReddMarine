## Why it was superseded

The newer work (in `../Strategy/`) is sharper on three things the older docs under-weight:

1. **Regulatory reality.** The ESD Prevention Association explicitly warns against any
   device marketed as a "safe to swim" green light, and certified ELCI breakers already
   do the protecting. The product has to be positioned as electrical-health / liability
   monitoring, riding on top of a certified breaker — not as a safety device itself.
2. **Sensing hardware.** Leakage current (1–30 mA) needs a residual-current monitor
   encircling all feed conductors, not a cheap clamp CT. That's an ~$80–600 BOM line,
   not ~$10, and it's the technical crux of the whole approach.
3. **Business model.** The per-dock subscription economics are thin (~$5.75/mo gross
   before opex) against a small market. That points toward a regional
   electrical-infrastructure / trade business with software as the moat, not a
   venture-scale SaaS. Which one it actually is gets decided by the 30-day builder
   interviews.

Nothing here is wrong to revisit — the roadmap structure, funding strategy, and
channel playbook are still useful reference. Just treat `../Strategy/` as the source
of truth.
